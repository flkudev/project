The references

Made slowly, on purpose.

01

Cut the plates

Each bridge is cut from a single block of German-silver, then hand-chamfered until the light breaks clean.

02

Finish by hand

Perlage, Côtes de Genève and polished screws, done by people who have done them ten thousand times.

03

Regulate for 120 days

Every movement is timed in five positions before it leaves the atelier. Nothing ships until it is right.
